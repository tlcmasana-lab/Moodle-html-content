Getting started

Six steps to complete the framework

Follow these steps in order. Open each one for what to do and where to go.

Sign in to the TLC Portal with your university credentials. Your profile stores enrolments, badges and submitted evidence. Placeholder: add portal URL.

Explore the catalogue of courses, workshops and programmes. Filter by competency theme to see the badges each pathway leads to.

Choose any three of the five themes. Use the theme picker in the Competency Themes section to record your selection.

Complete the required courses and activities in each chosen theme, then add a discipline-specific activity from the Activity Calculator.

Completing a pathway awards its competency badge. Six badges are required in total. Tick them off in the Progress Tracker.

Upload certificates, attendance records or reflections using the submission forms. The TLC team verifies your evidence before confirming your tier.
